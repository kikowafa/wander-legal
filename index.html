Common questions

Why does Wander need my location?

To work out where to start your route. Wander asks for location only while you're using the app, and we don't track you in the background.

Why are there no stops on my route?

Wander draws suggestions from live place data. Very short routes, rural areas, and quiet residential streets often have nothing worth stopping at nearby. Try a longer time budget or a route through a busier area.

Can I use Wander offline?

No. Wander needs an internet connection to build routes and look up places.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. It can't be undone.

The route sent me somewhere that didn't feel safe

Please tell us — email the route details and we'll look into it. Routes are generated automatically and aren't checked by a person, so we rely on reports like yours. Always use your own judgment about where it's safe to walk.
