Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026 Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Wander collects, why, and what control you have over it.

Wander is operated by Kiko Wafa, an individual based in New York, United States ("we," "us," "our").

We've tried to write this in plain language. If anything is unclear, email kikowafa@gmail.com and we'll explain it.

The short version

  • We collect your email address (to give you an account) and your location (to build walking routes).
  • We use location only while you're actively using the app. We do not track you in the background.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address — required to create an account and sign in.
  • Password — stored in hashed form by Firebase Authentication. We never see it.
  • Saved content — routes you save, places you favourite or hide, and preferences you set.
  • Profile information — a display name and, optionally, a profile picture.

Information collected automatically

  • Location. Wander requests location access only while the app is in use, to determine your starting point and build a route. We do not collect location when the app is closed or in the background. You can revoke this at any time in iOS Settings, though the app cannot generate routes without it.
  • Crash and error data. When something goes wrong, diagnostic information — device model, iOS version, and the technical details of the error — is sent to our error-reporting provider so we can fix it. We configure this to exclude your precise coordinates.
  • Basic usage data. IP address and app interaction data, used for security and troubleshooting.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, calendar, health data, or microphone input.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking technology.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about you.

How we use your information

Purpose Data used
Create and maintain your account Email, password
Generate walking routes and suggest stops Location
Save your routes, favourites and preferences Saved content, account ID
Diagnose crashes and fix bugs Crash data, device info
Protect against abuse and fraud IP address, usage data
Respond when you contact us Email, message content

We use your information for these purposes only. We do not use it to advertise to you, and we do not analyse it to build a marketing profile.

We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

This includes your location data, which we treat as sensitive information.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy, notify you before the change takes effect, and — where the law requires it — obtain your explicit opt-in consent first.

Third-party services we use

These providers process data on our behalf, under contract, and only to deliver the service:

Google Firebase (Google LLC)

Authentication, database, file storage and server functions. Stores your account, saved routes and preferences. Firebase Privacy · Google Privacy Policy

Google Maps Platform (Google LLC)

Maps SDK, Directions API, Places API and Geocoding API — used to display maps, calculate walking routes and find places. Location coordinates are sent to Google to perform these lookups. Your use of Wander is also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)

Crash and error reporting. Receives diagnostic data when the app encounters an error. Sentry Privacy Policy

We do not share your personal information with anyone else, except as described under "Legal disclosures" below.

How long we keep your information

  • Account data — for as long as your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Saved routes and preferences — same as above.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — up to 90 days.
  • Security and server logs — up to 12 months.

After you delete your account, residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before those backups are rotated out. They are not restored or accessed except where necessary for security or legal compliance.

Your choices and rights

Delete your account

Open Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently deletes your account, saved routes, favourites, preferences and profile picture. It cannot be undone.

Turn off location

iOS Settings → Wander → Location. The app can't generate routes without it, but you remain in control.

Access, correct or export your data

Email kikowafa@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or another US state with a privacy law

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to access and delete it, to correct inaccuracies, and to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling. We don't sell, share or use your data for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of — but the access, correction and deletion rights are all available at the contact address above. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

If you're in the UK, EU, or another region with comparable law

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Our legal bases are contract (to provide the service you asked for), consent (for location access, which you grant and can revoke), and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and fixing bugs). You may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

Children

Wander is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, email kikowafa@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Legal disclosures

We may disclose your information if we reasonably believe it's necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process or enforceable government request; to enforce our Terms of Service; to detect or prevent fraud or security issues; or to protect against harm to the rights, property or safety of our users or the public.

If Wander is ever involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may transfer to the acquiring party. We will notify you before that happens and before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers process data in the United States and other countries. If you use Wander from outside the US, your information will be transferred there. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, hashed password storage, and access rules restricting your data to your own account.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as required by law.

Links to other services

Wander may link to places, websites or services we don't operate. We're not responsible for their privacy practices — review their policies before sharing anything with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are material, we'll notify you in the app or by email before they take effect, and update the "Last updated" date above.
